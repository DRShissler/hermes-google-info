Hermes Personal

A private Google Workspace integration operated by Dan Shissler for his own use with Hermes Agent. This is not a public sign-up service or an official Google or Nous Research application.

Purpose

The integration lets Dan authorize his own Hermes Agent installation to work with his Google account. Depending on the permissions granted, it can help with email, calendars, files, documents, spreadsheets, and contacts. Google access is granted through OAuth; this website does not collect Google passwords or tokens.

Privacy and control

Google account access is limited by the permissions granted during authorization. Data used in agent tasks may be processed by the AI providers configured in Hermes. Access can be revoked in Google Account connections.

Read the privacy policy for details.
