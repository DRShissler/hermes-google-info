Privacy Policy

Hermes Personal — personal Google Workspace integration

This integration is operated by Dan Shissler for his own Google account and personal Hermes Agent installation. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Google data accessed and purpose

Only Google permissions approved during OAuth authorization are available. Depending on those permissions and the tasks requested, the integration may access email contents and metadata, calendar events, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, and contacts. Data is used to carry out personal assistance tasks, such as finding information, summarizing content, and making authorized changes.

Processing and disclosure

Hermes runs on the operator's computer. Information included in agent tasks may be sent to the AI model provider or other services configured for those tasks. This integration should not be understood as local-only processing. Those providers' own terms and privacy policies also apply. Google account data is not sold or used by the operator for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored by the operator's Hermes installation and are not published to this website or its GitHub repository. Task content may remain in local conversation history, logs, downloaded files, or task outputs. Information sent to external providers is subject to their retention policies. Access to the local computer and credentials must be protected by the operator.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Locally retained information remains until the operator deletes it. Google access can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously saved information. Local credentials, histories, and outputs can be deleted separately; external-provider copies must be managed under the relevant provider's controls.

This website

These static informational pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no application login, analytics scripts, or forms and do not receive Google OAuth credentials. GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests: drshissler@gmail.com. This policy should be updated if the integration's operation changes.
